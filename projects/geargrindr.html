GearGrindr

GearGrinr is one of my personal passion projects. I wanted to try out the new vibe-coding platforms, and as a results I created a tool that I've been personally wanted to exist for a while. An easy way to visualize how different bicycle drivetrain components, that are not officially compatible, actually would work together. The tool calculates the compatibility automatically, and creates a graph to show the possible drift in the drivetrain. I personally use the tool quite often, and seems to get constant traffic from other bike-minded people :).
