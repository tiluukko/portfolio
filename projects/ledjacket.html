Smart LED Jacket

As part of the AWE project, I designed and developed a wearable LED jacket as an HCI research platform for studying how visual signals can support human interaction with autonomous machines. The research explores applications such as improving safety and situational awareness in environments like mining, where people increasingly share workspaces with autonomous vehicles and machinery.

The jacket features 40 individually controllable LEDs, operated wirelessly through a mobile interface. I developed the wearable system as a functional research prototype, combining electronics, embedded control, wearable design and interaction design. A user study investigated how reliably people can detect body-worn visual signals while simultaneously performing another task, providing a basis for exploring how wearable interfaces could communicate information in demanding industrial environments.
