Emoji Keyboard for In-the-Wild Feedback Collection

As part of the REBOUND project, I designed an interactive Emoji Keyboard for participatory design and citizen engagement. The device was developed to make participation more intuitive and expressive, allowing people to communicate multiple emotional and cognitive responses rather than being limited to a single rating or predefined answer.

The 23-emoji interface was developed through an expert workshop and tested in a participatory installation exploring perceptions of wind turbine placement in natural landscapes. I developed the interaction concept and physical prototype, combining tangible interaction, custom fabrication and embedded electronics. A second iteration made the device more robust and fully standalone, improving its usability for participatory workshops and public installations.
