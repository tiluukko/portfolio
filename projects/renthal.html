Product concept - 2023

"Renthal" seat clamp

A product concept for a existing brand

Independently developed product concept as a personal exercise to practice my modeling and rendering skills.

I wanted to expand the manufacturer’s product range, while maintaining a strong aesthetic coherence, and create renderings that would be indistinguishable from the manufacturer’s current product imagery. The outcome was a series of renderings that closely mimic the manufacturer’s existing product visuals.
