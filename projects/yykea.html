Masters Thesis project - 2024

Yykeä Splitboard

As part of my Master’s thesis, I designed and prototyped a new splitboard model in collaboration with Finnish ski and snowboard manufacturer PUSU. The project focused on both the board design and exploring ways to streamline the manufacturing process through design and prototyping.

I was involved in the process from initial benchmarking and CAD design to developing molds, jigs and two functional prototypes with different core and sidewall materials. The boards were field-tested in the Lyngen Alps in Norway and Finnish Lapland, with the findings used to develop further design and manufacturing proposals for PUSU.
