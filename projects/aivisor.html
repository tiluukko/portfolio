Research Demo - 2025

Cough activated Face Visor

During the COVID-19 pandemic, I developed a dynamic face visor exploring how wearable technology could respond to environmental cues and support personal protection. The prototype automatically detects coughing and moves a transparent visor into a protective position, creating an immediate and tangible response to a potential exposure.

I was responsible for the technical implementation, programming and physical design. The prototype combines an Arduino Nano 33 BLE Sense, servo motors, custom 3D-printed components and a TinyML cough-detection model. The project explored smart PPE as an interactive system: rather than providing passive protection, the device actively responds to its surroundings and makes otherwise invisible environmental events perceptible to the wearer.
